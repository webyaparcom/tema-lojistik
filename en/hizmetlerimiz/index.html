Services

Services

From freight to warehousing

Solutions by the size and route of your cargo.

  • Full-load freight

    Door-to-door transport in a vehicle dedicated to one customer.

  • Part-load freight

    Shared, line-based shipments for small and medium consignments.

  • Warehousing

    Rack and pallet storage with in-out records.

  • Home moving

    Packing, transport and placement; a date-specific service.

  • International freight

    Road shipments to neighbouring countries with customs coordination.

  • Parcel and courier

    Parcel and document delivery under the postal services authorisation.
